### Parking Lot System

Functional Requirements: 
- Parking lot should have multiple floors
- One each floor there should be multiple spots
- Parking spots will be of different types - 'Motorcycle' , 'Car' , 'Truck'
- Different types of vehicles - 'Motorcycle' , 'Car' , 'Truck'
- System should be able to find suitable parking spots for each type of vehicle
- When the vehicle leaves, a ticket should get generated containing the duration of time for which vehicle was parked
- Total fare will get calculated based on the duration
- Then payment will happen using different payment methods
